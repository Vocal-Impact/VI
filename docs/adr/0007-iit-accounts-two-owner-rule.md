# 7. Services owned by IIT accounts, always with two or more owners

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-02)

## Context

The committee chose IIT Google accounts for service ownership. Those accounts are deactivated when students graduate.

## Decision

- **Owners:** every service (GitHub organisation, Vercel team, Neon organisation, Google Cloud project, email sender, ORS) has at least two committee owners, ideally from different study years.
- **Transferable containers:** organisations and teams are used so ownership can be handed over.
- **Handover:** the yearly process is in `docs/HANDOVER.md`.

## Consequences

- **Graduation risk:** no single graduation can lock the choir out.
- **Upkeep:** the handover checklist has to be run every year.
